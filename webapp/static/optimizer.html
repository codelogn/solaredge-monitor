Output vs peers, by hour of day

100% means "producing like its neighbours" in that hour, each hour judged against its own light level. A dip at particular hours is shade moving across the panel as the sun swings — a tree, a chimney, an aerial. A flat line sitting low all day is the hardware. This is the one comparison a single hour can't make, however much data it has.

Detected communication gaps

A gap means this optimizer's own reported timestamp jumped by more than ~2.5 poll intervals — it stopped sending fresh telemetry for that long. Cycles where our poller itself failed are excluded — but a gap can still be the inverter's uploads to SolarEdge stalling, which hits every optimizer at once (*10).
